## How to connect YuMI to RobotStudio
Start by connecting the computer with RobotStudio to the YuMI through the service port.

![service port](/media/images/ServicePort.jpg)


+ Open RobotStudio and press **One Click Connect** to connect to the robot. Use the defaults and continue.

![connect](/media/images/connect.png)

+ Through the FlexPendant set the YuMi to **auto mode** and in the same tab as before in RobotStudio (controller), click **Request Write Access** to be able to edit files on the YuMI's controller.
