## How to connect YuMI to RobotStudio
Start by connecting the computer with RobotStudio to the YuMI through the service port.

![service port](/media/images/ServicePort.jpg)


+ Open **RobotStudio** and proceed with the following steps:
    1. Press the **Controller** tab at the top of **RobotStudio**
    2. Click down the **Add Controller** options
    3. Press **One Click Connect...**
    4. Press **Log in as Default User**


![connect](/media/images/Oneclickconnect.png)
![defaultuser](/media/images/defaultuser.png)

+ Through the FlexPendant set the YuMi to **auto mode** by following these steps:
    1. Press the bottom right settings button
    2. Press the option at the top
    3. Choose **Auto**

![automode](/media/images/automode.png)

+ Then, in **RobotStudio**:

    1. Click the **RAPID** tab
    2. Click **Request Write Access**

![requestwrite](/media/images/requestwrite.png)

The YuMi robot should now be connected to the computer and you should be ready to add/modify RAPID code.
